Hydromind (Leisure Edition): Resort & Waterpark Hygiene GuardianA Multi-Modal Edge Telemetry & Real-Time Transparency Platform for Public Leisure Water Safety.1. Project Overview & ContextIn the post-pandemic era, public health consciousness has reached an all-time high. Visitors and families frequenting eco-resorts, waterparks, public swimming pools, and leisure lakes are increasingly concerned about water sanitation, chemical balancing, and hygiene maintenance.Currently, resort management relies on manual, periodic water testing kits (like chlorine drop tests or manual pH strips), which are prone to human error, infrequent, and completely opaque to guests. Visitors have no way of knowing the real-time sanitary status of the water they and their children are stepping into.2. Proposed SolutionHydromind (Leisure Edition) is an automated, edge-powered water hygiene guardian deployed directly at the edge of resort pools or water attraction intake streams.Instead of hiding water testing behind closed maintenance doors, Hydromind turns water safety into a transparent, guest-facing feature. The device continuously monitors physical telemetry and optical clarity, hosting a live, public dashboard via local Wi-Fi that guests can scan via QR code or check on their smartphones before entering the water.3. Core Features & Technical ArchitectureMulti-Modal Sensor Fusion: Combines physical telemetry (water temperature via a DS18B20 probe and suspended particle density/cloudiness via an analog turbidity sensor) with computer vision (optical color profiling via an ESP32-CAM frame parser mapping RGB to Hue angles).Bandwidth-Optimized Edge Telemetry: Extracts critical clarity metrics on the microcontroller and transmits lightweight JSON payloads to a local FastAPI backend, avoiding heavy, resource-wasting video streaming.Predictive Hygiene Scoring: Powered by a Scikit-Learn machine learning pipeline (RandomForestRegressor) that computes an instant, high-accuracy $0–100$ Water Quality Index (WQI) categorized into clear operational tiers (Optimal, Caution, and Closed/Hazardous).Public Transparency Dashboard: Serves a sleek, real-time mobile-friendly web interface displaying live temperature, turbidity levels, visual proof frames, and active safety badges.4.
